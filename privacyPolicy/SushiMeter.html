🍣 Privacy Policy – Sushi Meter

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Thank you for using Sushi Meter. Your privacy is very important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about how your information is handled.

In short:
Sushi Meter does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All data remains locally on your device. The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob, which collects device and usage information, and we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR-compliant consent. We also use Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) for technical crash diagnostics and anonymous, aggregated telemetry to monitor application stability and performance without identifying individual users.

1. What This App Does

Sushi Meter is a productivity and utility tool that allows you to:

  • Keep track of the number of sushi pieces you eat during your meals
  • View your local counting history

The app runs offline-first and is designed to be simple, secure, and respectful of your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local App Data

Sushi Meter stores your sushi counts and local usage history directly on your device. This data is kept solely within the application's local storage and is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with external servers.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To serve ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited data, which includes:

  • Advertising ID (provided by Android, which you can reset in your device settings)
  • Device metadata (device model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • App usage data related to ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Network information (carrier, connection type)

For users in the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA), a GDPR-compliant consent management mechanism (Google UMP SDK) is presented before any data is used for personalized advertisements.

2.3 Crash Reporting and Diagnostics (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

To detect, diagnose, and resolve technical errors and ensure application stability, Sushi Meter integrates Firebase Crashlytics. When a crash or fatal error occurs, the following diagnostic information is transmitted:

  • Crash logs and stack traces detailing the exact point of failure in the software code.
  • Device state at the time of the crash (device manufacturer and model, operating system version, orientation, free RAM, and storage space).
  • Technical Firebase Installation UUID (Crashlytics Installation UUID), a randomly generated identifier used solely to differentiate individual crashes without identifying the user or device owner.

Firebase Crashlytics does not collect personally identifying information (such as your name, email, or IP address) or any sushi count history. Processing is based on our legitimate interest in maintaining software quality, reliability, and security.

2.4 App Telemetry and Usage Metrics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To understand aggregate feature usage patterns, measure general app performance, and improve the user experience, Sushi Meter collects anonymous, aggregated metrics via Google Firebase Analytics under strict GDPR privacy controls:

  • No User Identification: The user ID feature (setUserId()) is disabled. No persistent identifiers or personal user profiles are generated, maintained, or stored.
  • No Personal Data: Sushi counts and local history are strictly processed locally and are never sent to Firebase Analytics.
  • No Advertising ID or Ad Personalization: Collection of the Google Advertising ID (GAID) is disabled and never associated with analytics events.
  • Anonymous Usage Events: Only aggregated, anonymized events (such as app opens and feature interactions) are collected, without any personally identifiable or sensitive information.

Data is processed on the legal basis of our legitimate interest in optimizing application usability, reliability, and stability, protected by full pseudonymization and data aggregation.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and support the app's functionality:

  • App Functionality: Local data is processed only to manage your sushi counts and display your meal history on your device.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Bug Fixing: Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and device diagnostics to identify, debug, and fix software defects.
  • Aggregate Usage Analytics: Firebase Analytics processes anonymous, aggregated feature usage metrics to improve application usability, performance, and stability.
  • Advertising: AdMob uses advertising identifiers to serve, limit, and personalize ads, subject to your choices and consent.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics)
    Provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited
    Purpose: Error diagnosis, crash reporting, application stability, and aggregated feature telemetry
    Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
    Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    Provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited
    Purpose: To display in-app advertisements
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com
    Mandatory Google Disclosure: Please review how Google processes data when you use this app at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

5.1 No Remote Storage

We do not operate backend servers. We never receive, store, or process your counts or history on any servers owned or operated by us.

5.2 Local Storage

All data is saved locally on your device. It is isolated within the app's secure sandbox environment, meaning other apps on your device cannot access it.

5.3 Security Measures

  • App Sandbox: Android's security architecture ensures your local data remains private.
  • Encrypted Connections: Network communication for ads, crash diagnostics (Crashlytics), and telemetry (Analytics) is fully encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5.4 Data Retention

All local data (sushi counts and history) is stored persistently on your device until you manually clear it or uninstall the app. You can clear this data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Sushi Meter > Storage > Clear Data. AdMob data retention is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash logs for up to 90 days before deletion, and Firebase Analytics metrics are aggregated and retained according to standard Google Firebase policies.

6. Permissions Required

The app requests the following permission:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to load advertisements, transmit crash reports (Crashlytics), send anonymous telemetry (Firebase Analytics), and check consent choices.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete App Data: You can clear all counting history at any time by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Ad Consent Choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device's settings. For users in the EU/EEA, you can change or withdraw your consent preferences at any time through the ad consent settings inside the app.

8. Children's Privacy

Sushi Meter is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. AdMob serves ads in accordance with Google's family and content rating policies.

9. Additional Information for European Union Users (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Union or the European Economic Area (EEA), the following provisions apply to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

9.1 Data Controller

The Data Controller is Alessandro Barbieri. For any privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact the Data Controller at: ale.ialex5@yahoo.it.

9.2 Legal Bases for Processing

We process information under the following valid legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To store your sushi-piece counts and local usage history on your device as requested. This processing is strictly local.
  • User Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob, gathered through the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For maintaining app security, preventing fraud, diagnosing crashes and fixing software errors via Firebase Crashlytics, analyzing aggregate and anonymous usage trends via Firebase Analytics (without user ID tracking and without personal data), and conducting basic ad operations (such as frequency capping).

9.3 International Data Transfers

Information processed by third-party services like Google AdMob and Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics) may be transferred to, and stored in, countries outside the EEA, including the United States. Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework to guarantee a compliant level of data protection.

9.4 Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), and the right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) at any time. To exercise these rights (where applicable, though your data is stored only locally), you can clear your app data or contact the Data Controller's email.

9.5 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

10. Additional Information for California Residents (CCPA)

For residents of California, USA, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app collects only locally stored count data. Third-party ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage statistics as detailed in Section 2.2.
  • You have the right to access and delete your local data (which can be completed directly by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app).

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will always be available at this same link. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates when the policy was last updated.

🍣 Informativa sulla Privacy – Sushi Meter

Data di entrata in vigore: 25 Novembre 2026

Grazie per aver scelto Sushi Meter. La tua privacy è molto importante per noi e ci impegniamo ad essere trasparenti su come vengono gestite le tue informazioni.

In breve:
Sushi Meter non raccoglie, archivia o condivide alcun dato personale su server esterni. Tutti i dati rimangono localmente sul tuo dispositivo. L'app mostra annunci pubblicitari tramite Google AdMob, che raccoglie informazioni sul dispositivo e sull'utilizzo, e utilizziamo la User Messaging Platform (UMP) di Google per raccogliere il consenso conforme al GDPR. Utilizziamo inoltre Google Firebase (Crashlytics e Analytics) per la diagnosi tecnica dei crash e per raccogliere metriche di telemetria aggregate e anonime per monitorare la stabilità e le prestazioni dell'app, senza tracciare i singoli utenti.

1. Cosa Fa Questa App

Sushi Meter è uno strumento di utilità e produttività che ti consente di:

  • Tenere traccia del numero di pezzi di sushi che mangi durante i tuoi pasti
  • Visualizzare la cronologia locale dei tuoi conteggi

L'app funziona principalmente offline ed è progettata per essere semplice, sicura e rispettosa della tua privacy.

2. Informazioni che Raccogliamo

2.1 Dati Locali dell'App

Sushi Meter memorizza i conteggi dei pezzi di sushi e la cronologia locale direttamente sul tuo dispositivo. Questi dati rimangono esclusivamente all'interno dell'archiviazione locale dell'applicazione e non vengono mai caricati, trasmessi o condivisi con server esterni.

2.2 Pubblicità (Google AdMob)

Questa app utilizza Google AdMob per visualizzare annunci pubblicitari. Per pubblicare gli annunci, Google AdMob potrebbe raccogliere automaticamente informazioni limitate, tra cui:

  • ID pubblicitario (fornito da Android, che puoi reimpostare nelle impostazioni del dispositivo)
  • Metadati del dispositivo (modello, versione del sistema operativo, risoluzione dello schermo)
  • Dati sull'utilizzo dell'app correlati a impressioni e interazioni con gli annunci
  • Posizione approssimativa (basata sull'indirizzo IP)
  • Informazioni sulla rete (operatore telefonico, tipo di connessione)

Per gli utenti dell'Unione Europea (UE) e dello Spazio Economico Europeo (SEE), viene presentato un meccanismo di gestione del consenso conforme al GDPR (Google UMP SDK) prima che i dati vengano utilizzati per annunci personalizzati.

2.3 Segnalazione Crash e Diagnostica (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

Per rilevare, diagnosticare e risolvere errori tecnici e garantire la stabilità dell'applicazione, Sushi Meter integra Firebase Crashlytics. In caso di arresto anomalo o errore critico, vengono raccolte e trasmesse le seguenti informazioni tecniche:

  • Log di crash e stack trace relativi all'errore, che consentono di identificare con precisione la riga di codice e il componente interessato dal problema.
  • Stato tecnico del dispositivo al momento del crash (produttore, modello, versione del sistema operativo, orientamento dello schermo, memoria RAM disponibile e spazio di archiviazione).
  • UUID tecnico di installazione Firebase (Crashlytics Installation UUID), generato in modo casuale per distinguere le singole occorrenze di crash senza identificare l'utente o il dispositivo.

Firebase Crashlytics non raccoglie dati personali identificativi (quali nome, cognome, indirizzo email o IP) né la cronologia dei conteggi del sushi. La base giuridica del trattamento è il legittimo interesse a garantire l'affidabilità, la sicurezza e il funzionamento dell'applicazione.

2.4 Metriche di Utilizzo e Telemetria (Google Firebase Analytics)

Per comprendere l'uso generale delle funzionalità dell'applicazione e ottimizzare l'esperienza d'uso, Sushi Meter raccoglie metriche in modalità anonima e aggregata tramite Google Firebase Analytics, nel rispetto delle norme GDPR:

  • Nessun Tracciamento dell'Utente: L'identificatore utente (setUserId()) è disabilitato. Nessun identificatore personale, persistente o profilo individuale viene generato o memorizzato.
  • Nessun Dato Personale: I conteggi del sushi e la cronologia locale sono elaborati unicamente in locale sul dispositivo e non vengono mai inviati a Firebase Analytics.
  • Disattivazione Personalizzazione Annunci: La raccolta del Google Advertising ID (GAID) è disattivata e non viene associata agli eventi analitici.
  • Eventi Anonimi di Utilizzo: Vengono raccolti esclusivamente eventi aggregati e anonimizzati (come aperture dell'app e interazioni con le funzionalità), senza dati personali o sensibili.

La base giuridica del trattamento è il legittimo interesse volto al miglioramento continuo e alla stabilità del servizio, in virtù della pseudonimizzazione completa e dell'assenza di profilazione individualizzata.
