🔒 Privacy Policy – ICS Importer

Effective Date: February 7, 2026

Thank you for using ICS Importer. Your privacy is very important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about how your information is handled.

In short:
ICS Importer helps you import calendar events from .ics files into your Google Calendar. The app does not store your calendar data or personal information on external servers. All data flows directly between your device and Google's services. The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob, which collects device and usage information, and we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR-compliant consent.

1. What This App Does

ICS Importer is a productivity tool that allows you to:

  • Import calendar events from .ics (iCalendar) files into your Google Calendar
  • Preview events before importing them
  • Automatically import events to a default calendar of your choice

The app is designed to be simple, secure, and respectful of your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we access:

  • Email address and profile name: Used for authentication purposes only. This information is not stored by our app and is handled securely by Google's authentication system.
  • Google Calendar access: Required to retrieve your calendar list and create events in your selected Google Calendar.

2.2 Calendar Data

The app accesses your list of Google Calendars (names and IDs) and the calendar events being imported (titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, attendees). This data is processed dynamically in memory and is not permanently stored or saved on any servers owned by us.

2.3 Local Preferences

The app stores your preferences (auto-import enabled/disabled, and default calendar selection ID and name) locally on your device in Android SharedPreferences. This data is not transmitted to external servers.

2.4 File Access

When you open an .ics file, the app reads the file contents temporarily in memory to extract event data. No permanent copy of the file is saved.

2.5 Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To serve ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited data, which includes:

  • Advertising ID (provided by Android, which you can reset in your device settings)
  • Device metadata (device model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • App usage data related to ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Network information (carrier, connection type)

Important: Google user data accessed by Google APIs (such as calendar data or account information) is never used or shared for advertising purposes. AdMob data is collected independently. For users in the EU/EEA, a GDPR-compliant consent management mechanism (Google UMP SDK) is presented before any data is used for personalized advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and support the app's functionality:

  • App Functionality: Google sign-in is used to authenticate you, and the Calendar API is used to list your calendars and create events. Preferences are used locally to remember your settings.
  • Advertising: AdMob uses advertising identifiers to serve, limit, and personalize ads, subject to your choices and consent.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In API
    Provider: Google LLC
    Purpose: User authentication
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Calendar API
    Provider: Google LLC
    Purpose: Retrieve calendar list and create events
    OAuth Scopes Used:
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to create and manage events
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly — to read the list of calendars
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    Provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited
    Purpose: To display in-app advertisements
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com
    Mandatory Google Disclosure: Please review how Google processes data when you use this app at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

5.1 No Remote Storage

We do not operate backend servers. All calendar data flows directly from your device to Google's servers via secure HTTPS connections. We never receive, store, or process your calendar data or account credentials on any servers owned or operated by us.

5.2 Local Storage

Preferences (auto-import, default calendar ID) are stored unencrypted in Android SharedPreferences, as they contain no sensitive personal information.

5.3 Security Measures

  • Encrypted Communications: All network communication is fully encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • OAuth 2.0: Industry-standard authentication; the app does not store or handle your Google password.
  • App Sandbox: Android's isolation environment prevents other apps from accessing local preferences.

5.4 Data Retention

Calendar data and account details are processed in memory during the active session and are discarded immediately upon closing the app or completing the import. Local preferences remain stored persistently until you clear app data or uninstall the app. You can clear this data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > ICS Importer > Storage > Clear Data. AdMob data retention is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions Required

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to communicate with Google Calendar APIs, load ads, and check consent choices.
  • Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): (Android 13+) Used to notify you when auto-import completes. You can deny this; auto-import will still function normally.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • Sign Out: You can sign out of your Google account at any time within the app.
  • Revoke Access: You can revoke the app's access to your Google Calendar at any time in your Google Account security settings.
  • Delete App Data: You can wipe all local preferences by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Ad Consent Choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device's settings. For users in the EU/EEA, you can change or withdraw your consent preferences at any time through the ad consent settings inside the app.

8. Children's Privacy

ICS Importer is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. AdMob serves ads in accordance with Google's family and content rating policies.

9. Additional Information for European Union Users (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Union or the European Economic Area (EEA), the following provisions apply to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • User Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob, gathered through the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For maintaining app security, preventing fraud, and conducting basic ad operations (such as frequency capping).

Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework to guarantee a compliant level of data protection. As a data subject, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), and the right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) at any time. 9.5 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

10. Additional Information for California Residents (CCPA)

For residents of California, USA, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The app accesses email and calendar data for functionality, processing it in-memory. Third-party ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage statistics as detailed in Section 2.5.
  • You have the right to access and delete your local data (which can be completed directly by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app).

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

🔒 Informativa sulla Privacy – ICS Importer

Data di entrata in vigore: 7 Febbraio 2026

Grazie per aver scelto ICS Importer. La tua privacy è molto importante per noi e ci impegniamo ad essere trasparenti su come vengono gestite le tue informazioni.

In breve:
ICS Importer ti aiuta a importare eventi di calendario da file .ics nel tuo Google Calendar. L'app non memorizza i tuoi dati del calendario o le informazioni personali su server esterni. Tutti i dati fluiscono direttamente tra il tuo dispositivo e i servizi di Google. L'app mostra annunci pubblicitari tramite Google AdMob, che raccoglie informazioni sul dispositivo e sull'utilizzo, e utilizziamo la User Messaging Platform (UMP) di Google per raccogliere il consenso conforme al GDPR.

1. Cosa Fa Questa App

ICS Importer è uno strumento di produttività che ti consente di:

  • Importare eventi del calendario da file .ics (iCalendar) nel tuo Google Calendar
  • Visualizzare in anteprima gli eventi prima di importarli
  • Importare automaticamente gli eventi in un calendario predefinito a tua scelta

L'app è progettata per essere semplice, sicura e rispettosa della tua privacy.

2. Informazioni che Raccogliamo

2.1 Informazioni sull'Account Google

Quando accedi con Google, accediamo a:

  • Indirizzo email e nome del profilo: Utilizzati solo a scopo di autenticazione. Queste informazioni non sono memorizzate dall'app e sono gestite in modo sicuro dal sistema di autenticazione di Google.
  • Accesso a Google Calendar: Richiesto per recuperare l'elenco dei tuoi calendari e creare eventi nel calendario da te selezionato.

2.2 Dati del Calendario

L'app accede all'elenco dei tuoi Google Calendar (nomi e ID) e agli eventi da importare (titoli, date, orari, descrizioni, luoghi, partecipanti). Questi dati sono elaborati temporaneamente in memoria e non vengono archiviati in modo permanente su server di nostra proprietà.

2.3 Preferenze Locali

L'app memorizza le tue preferenze (importazione automatica abilitata/disabilitata e ID e nome del calendario predefinito) localmente sul tuo dispositivo nelle SharedPreferences di Android. Questi dati non vengono trasmessi a server esterni.

2.4 Accesso ai File

Quando apri un file .ics, l'app legge il contenuto del file temporaneamente in memoria per estrarre i dati degli eventi. Non viene salvata alcuna copia permanente del file sul dispositivo.

2.5 Pubblicità (Google AdMob)

Questa app utilizza Google AdMob per visualizzare annunci pubblicitari. Per pubblicare gli annunci, Google AdMob potrebbe raccogliere automaticamente informazioni limitate, tra cui:

  • ID pubblicitario (fornito da Android, che puoi reimpostare nelle impostazioni del dispositivo)
  • Metadati del dispositivo (modello, versione del sistema operativo, risoluzione dello schermo)
  • Dati sull'utilizzo dell'app correlati a impressioni e interazioni con gli annunci
  • Posizione approssimativa (basata sull'indirizzo IP)
  • Informazioni sulla rete (operatore telefonico, tipo di connessione)

Importante: I dati utente di Google a cui si accede tramite le API di Google (come i dati del calendario o le informazioni sull'account) non vengono mai utilizzati o condivisi per scopi pubblicitari. I dati di AdMob sono raccolti in modo indipendente. Per gli utenti dell'UE/SEE, viene presentato un modulo conforme al GDPR (Google UMP SDK) per il consenso prima dell'uso di annunci personalizzati.

3. Come Utilizziamo le Tue Informazioni

Le tue informazioni vengono utilizzate esclusivamente per fornire e supportare le funzionalità dell'app:

  • Funzionalità dell'App: L'accesso con Google viene utilizzato per autenticarti, mentre le API di Calendar vengono utilizzate per elencare i calendari e creare eventi. Le preferenze vengono utilizzate localmente per ricordare le tue impostazioni.
  • Pubblicità: AdMob utilizza gli identificatori pubblicitari per pubblicare, limitare e personalizzare gli annunci, in conformità con le tue scelte e il tuo consenso.
