🔒 Privacy Policy – DriveTracker

Effective Date: February 2, 2026

Thank you for using DriveTracker. Your privacy is very important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about how your information is handled.

In short:
DriveTracker operates as an offline-first app and stores location and trip data locally on your device. The app collects foreground and background location data to record your trips even when the screen is off or the app is closed. The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob, which collects device and usage information, and we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR-compliant consent.

1. What This App Does

DriveTracker is a productivity and utility tool that allows you to:

  • Record your car trips and view them on a map
  • Calculate trip statistics such as speed, distance, and duration
  • Keep a local logbook of your driving routes with readable addresses

The app is designed to be secure, private, and to work completely offline.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data (Foreground & Background)

To enable the recording of your car trips, calculate driving statistics, and draw your route on the map, DriveTracker collects location data. This collection occurs in the foreground while you use the app, and also in the background (when the app is closed or the screen is off), provided that you have actively started a recording session by pressing the "Start Tracking" button. Location coordinates are used locally with the Android Geocoder and via secure API requests with the LocationIQ API to translate your location into readable addresses (reverse geocoding) for your trip logs. We also use the TomTom API to retrieve routing and estimated travel time (ETA) statistics for your trips. Location data is stored locally in the application's private database and is never transmitted to any external servers owned by us.

2.2 Activity Recognition

The app requests permission to detect when you are driving or in a vehicle. This helps suggest starting a trip recording. No activity recognition data is stored, exported, or transmitted; it is processed momentarily on your device to enhance your user experience.

2.3 Bluetooth Connection Data

The app requests Bluetooth permissions to detect when your device connects to device accessories (specifically your car's hands-free or Bluetooth audio system). This is used solely to automatically suggest starting a trip tracking session. No Bluetooth device identifiers, pairing logs, or connection history are stored, uploaded, or shared.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To serve ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited data, which includes:

  • Advertising ID (provided by Android, which you can reset in your device settings)
  • Device metadata (device model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • App usage data related to ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Network information (carrier, connection type)

For users in the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA), a GDPR-compliant consent management mechanism (Google UMP SDK) is presented before any data is used for personalized advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and support the app's functionality:

  • App Functionality: Location and activity data are processed locally to map your routes and calculate speed, distance, and duration.
  • Reverse Geocoding: To translate coordinates of trip start and end points into readable addresses using Android Geocoder and LocationIQ.
  • Estimated Travel Time: To calculate ETA and routing statistics using the TomTom API.
  • Bluetooth Auto-Start: To suggest starting a recording session when your device connects to your car's Bluetooth system.
  • Advertising: AdMob uses advertising identifiers to serve, limit, and personalize ads, subject to your choices and consent.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Maps SDK
    Provider: Google LLC
    Purpose: To render maps and display routes
  • Google Play Services
    Provider: Google LLC
    Purpose: To facilitate high-accuracy location tracking
  • LocationIQ API & Android Geocoder
    Purpose: To translate GPS coordinates of your trip's start and end points into readable addresses
    LocationIQ Privacy Policy: https://locationiq.com/privacy
  • TomTom API
    Purpose: To calculate estimated travel time (ETA) and routing statistics for your trips
    TomTom Privacy Policy: https://www.tomtom.com/privacy/
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    Provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited
    Purpose: To display in-app advertisements
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com
    Mandatory Google Disclosure: Please review how Google processes data when you use this app at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

5.1 No Remote Storage

We do not operate backend servers. All trip coordinates, statistics, and location history are processed and saved solely in a local database on your device. We never receive, store, or process your coordinates or trip history on any servers owned or operated by us.

5.2 Local Storage

All trip logs are saved locally in the app's secure SQLite/Room database. This database is sandbox-isolated and protected by the Android operating system, preventing other apps from accessing your location log.

5.3 Security Measures

  • App Sandbox: Android's isolation architecture protects your local database from external access.
  • Encrypted Connections: Network communication for map tiles, geocoding APIs, TomTom API, and ads is fully encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5.4 Data Retention

All local trip records are stored persistently on your device until you choose to delete them, clear the app data, or uninstall the app. You can delete all logs at any time via Android Settings > Apps > DriveTracker > Storage > Clear Data. AdMob data retention is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions Required

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required to obtain trip coordinates and calculate speed and distance.
  • Background Location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Required to record your routes while the screen is off or the app is closed.
  • Activity Recognition (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): Required to detect when you are inside a vehicle.
  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Required to detect Bluetooth connections to your car's hands-free system to suggest starting a trip tracking session.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): Required to keep location tracking active in the background while you navigate away or turn off the screen.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to display a persistent notification with tracking controls during an active recording session.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to load map tiles, communicate with geocoding/TomTom APIs, display advertisements, and check consent choices.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete App Data: You can wipe all recorded trips by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Permissions Control: You can revoke location (including background location), Bluetooth, and activity recognition permissions at any time in your Android system settings.
  • Ad Consent Choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device's settings. For users in the EU/EEA, you can change or withdraw your consent preferences at any time through the ad consent settings inside the app.

8. Children's Privacy

DriveTracker is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. AdMob serves ads in accordance with Google's family and content rating policies.

🔒 Informativa sulla Privacy – DriveTracker

  • Registrare i tuoi viaggi in auto e visualizzarli su una mappa

  • Tenere un registro locale dei percorsi di guida con indirizzi leggibili

L'app è progettata per essere sicura, privata e per funzionare completamente offline.

2. Informazioni che Raccogliamo

2.1 Danti sulla Posizione (In primo piano e in Background)

Per consentire la registrazione dei tuoi viaggi in auto, calcolare le statistiche di guida e tracciare il tuo percorso sulla mappa, DriveTracker raccoglie dati sulla posizione. Questa raccolta avviene in primo piano mentre usi l'app, e anche in background (quando l'app è chiusa o lo schermo è spento), a condizione che tu abbia avviato attivamente una sessione di registrazione premendo il pulsante "Inizia Tracciamento". Le coordinate di posizione vengono utilizzate localmente con il Geocoder di Android e tramite richieste API sicure con l'API di LocationIQ per tradurre la tua posizione in indirizzi leggibili (reverse geocoding) per i tuoi registri di viaggio. I dati sulla posizione sono memorizzati localmente nel database privato dell'applicazione e non vengono mai trasmessi a server esterni di nostra proprietà.

L'app richiede l'autorizzazione per rilevare quando sei alla guida o all'interno di un veicolo. Questo aiuta a suggerire di iniziare la registrazione di un viaggio.
