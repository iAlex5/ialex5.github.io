🔒 Privacy Policy – RogUpdater

Effective Date: May 5, 2025

Thank you for using RogUpdater. Your privacy is very important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about how your information is handled.

In short:
RogUpdater does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All operations occur locally on your device. The app connects directly to official firmware sources to check for updates. The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob, which collects device and usage information, and we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR-compliant consent.

1. What This App Does

RogUpdater is a utility tool that allows you to:

  • Check for official firmware updates for Asus ROG devices
  • Download firmware updates from official sources

The app is designed to be simple, secure, and respectful of your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local and Update Data

RogUpdater does not directly collect or store any personal information. When checking for updates, the app connects directly to official Asus firmware servers to fetch release links. No user data, device IDs, or download history are stored on any developer-owned servers.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To serve ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited data, which includes:

  • Advertising ID (provided by Android, which you can reset in your device settings)
  • Device metadata (device model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • App usage data related to ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Network information (carrier, connection type)

For users in the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA), a GDPR-compliant consent management mechanism (Google UMP SDK) is presented before any data is used for personalized advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and support the app's functionality:

  • App Functionality: Network connections are made exclusively to query official update servers and fetch firmware download links as requested by you.
  • Advertising: AdMob uses advertising identifiers to serve, limit, and personalize ads, subject to your choices and consent.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Official Firmware Servers (Asus)
    Purpose: To check for and download official ROG updates
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    Provider: Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited
    Purpose: To display in-app advertisements
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com
    Mandatory Google Disclosure: Please review how Google processes data when you use this app at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

5.1 No Remote Storage

We do not operate backend servers. We never receive, store, or process any of your hardware data or update history on any servers owned or operated by us.

5.2 Local Storage

Any settings or cache related to the update checker are saved locally on your device within the app's secure sandbox environment, meaning other apps cannot access them.

5.3 Security Measures

  • App Sandbox: Android's security architecture ensures your local environment remains private.
  • Encrypted Connections: Network communication for checking updates and loading ads is fully encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5.4 Data Retention

Because no personal data is collected or stored on external servers, there is no personal data to retain. Local cache and configuration settings are retained until you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings. AdMob data retention is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions Required

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to communicate with official firmware update sources, load advertisements, and check consent choices.
  • Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to notify you when new official firmware updates are found for your Asus ROG device.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete App Data: You can clear all cached configuration at any time by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Ad Consent Choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device's settings. For users in the EU/EEA, you can change or withdraw your consent preferences at any time through the ad consent settings inside the app.

8. Children's Privacy

RogUpdater is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. AdMob serves ads in accordance with Google's family and content rating policies.
